Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grandma Kitchen Publisher (the “Service”) handles information when a creator connects and publishes to an authorized social account.

1. Information we process

  • Account identifiers and basic profile information returned by the platform, such as an open identifier, username, display name, avatar, and creator publishing capabilities.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to maintain the connection you authorize.
  • Videos, covers, titles, captions, hashtags, privacy selections, and other publishing settings that you choose.
  • Publication status, platform response identifiers, timestamps, retry counts, and error information needed to show delivery progress and troubleshoot failures.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate the connected account, display current creator capabilities, prepare the publication you request, send it through the official platform API after your confirmation, show its status, and protect or troubleshoot the Service.

3. Sharing

We send selected content and publishing settings to the destination platform you choose, such as TikTok. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. A platform processes submitted information under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Storage and retention

The Service is operated as a controlled desktop workflow. Credentials are stored in project-scoped configuration and are not intentionally exposed in the user interface or publication records. Connection credentials are kept until they are replaced, revoked, or removed. Publication records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for history, status, retry, security, and troubleshooting.

5. Your choices

You decide which account to connect, which content to publish, its privacy level, and whether to enable available interaction or disclosure settings. You may revoke the Service’s access through the destination platform. You may also request deletion of locally stored connection and publication information through the contact channel below.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect credentials and publication data. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children and is intended for creators who are eligible to control and publish to the connected accounts.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy as the Service evolves. The effective date above identifies the current version.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests may be sent through the developer’s public GitHub profile.
